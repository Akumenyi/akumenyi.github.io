Open with the claim, not the throat-clearing. The first paragraph is what people
see on the index card and in link previews, so make it carry the argument.

## Subheadings break the argument into moves

Regular paragraphs, **bold** for emphasis, *italics* for journal titles and terms
of art, and [links](https://example.org) wherever a claim rests on a source.

> Pull quotes are for the sentence you would want someone to screenshot.

- Bulleted lists for enumerations
- Keep them short; prose carries nuance better

Reference your own work by DOI so the claim is checkable:
[Impact of Stratospheric Aerosol Injection on Precipitation Extremes Across Africa](https://doi.org/10.1088/2752-5295/adee3c).

Images sit on their own line:

![Description of the figure](/assets/img/climate-banner.jpg)

Close with what the reader should do or watch for next.
