The SAI evidence ledger for climate vulnerable countries

Peer-reviewed projections concentrate both the largest benefits and the most serious harms in the same countries.

Projected benefits

~400,000 fewer heat and cold deaths per year

1°C of cooling in a 2.5°C world; gains concentrated in hotter, poorer regions. Wide uncertainty range.

Harding et al. 2024, PNAS

Lower inter-country income inequality

Cooling disproportionately lifts hot tropical economies in every model specification tested.

Harding et al. 2020, Nature Communications

Fewer intense-rainfall extremes in West and Central Africa

Reduced frequency and intensity where extremes would otherwise intensify; strong subregional variation.

Quagraine et al. 2025, Environ. Res.: Climate

Projected harms and risks

Malaria protection nullified for ~1 billion people

Cooling under high emissions could undo a projected reduction in highest-risk population by 2070; ~200M more in West Africa alone.

Carlson et al. 2022, Nature Communications

Monsoon drying and rainfall disruption

Drying alongside cooling threatens rainfed agriculture, grazing and hydropower in parts of Africa.

Bonou et al. 2023, Earth's Future; ARISE/GLENS studies

Termination shock and governance failure

Abrupt stop after deterred mitigation would unload decades of masked warming on the least protected.

Felgenhauer et al. 2025; McLaren 2025
